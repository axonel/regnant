# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Regnant respectful for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and receive technical feedback professionally.
- Focus criticism on code, decisions and behaviour, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials or personal information.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them promptly.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, intimidation and publication of private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and comments that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently exclude contributors for serious or repeated violations.

Report concerns privately through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting feature for security matters or contact an organization administrator for conduct matters.
